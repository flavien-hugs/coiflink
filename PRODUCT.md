# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CoifLink is used by salon managers in Côte d’Ivoire and West Africa while they run daily operations. They need to create appointments, maintain customer records, manage staff and services, record payments, and consult activity without slowing down reception or service.

## Product Purpose

CoifLink replaces paper notes, calls, and fragmented messages with one dependable workspace for salon operations. Success means a manager can understand the current state of the salon and complete a routine action quickly, even on modest hardware and a busy day.

## Brand Personality

Simple, reassuring, and fast. The interface speaks in direct, practical French and keeps operational status and next actions easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense back-office screens that hide essential actions, ambiguous controls, unnecessary decoration, and low-contrast data tables that are difficult to use during a busy service.

## Design Principles

- Put the next operational action and its status in clear view.
- Preserve a consistent vocabulary across lists, forms, and dashboards.
- Make common tasks easy to scan and complete without training.
- Keep data tables calm, readable, and predictable as records grow.
- Prefer explicit feedback and accessible defaults over clever interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for interface text and controls. Every interactive element must be usable by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator, semantic labels, and no interaction that relies only on color. Respect reduced-motion preferences where motion is introduced.
